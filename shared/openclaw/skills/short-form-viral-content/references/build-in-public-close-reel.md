# Build-in-public close reel

A shape for a service close, a client visit, or a numbered "Day N" build-in-public short. It applies Noe's three levers. It does not replace them.

The operating checklist is in `SKILL.md` (template **T8**, and the close-reel section). This file is the worked example and the account evidence.

## Worked example

@a13x_duke (cA13X / Alex), Day 5, Inti Peruvian Restaurant.

About 61 seconds. 606.3K views as of 2026-09-29. The caption tells a "longtime customer, now their web client" story. The edit does something else: it teaches the shots, then shows the client and the price.

| Sec | Visual | On-screen text | Job |
|-----|--------|----------------|-----|
| 0–1 | Face, hand up (five fingers) | day 5 and today we made | Unfinished scoreboard. Scroll stops on the missing number |
| 2–4 | Same, dollar card appears | Day 5: $1,550 / let me show you how this video is gonna be | Pays the hook, promises a recipe |
| 5–23 | Car, storefront, door, walk-in, left-to-right pan | step 1 outside, step 2 front, step 3 walk in, "most important step" pan | Retention: new place every step, label stays up |
| 26–29 | Those shots, cut together | if you did it correctly, it should come out like this | Raw → finished is a second hook |
| 31–44 | At the table, laptop, site on screen | client: more pictures, on the phone, "I like it" | Proof is overheard, not narrated |
| 45–54 | Same table | $1,300 to own it, then $250 maintenance | The number is spoken over the work |
| 54–60 | Black card | Day 5: $1,500 / just like that, no hesitation, easy | Loop restarts on the result |

## Do not copy the broken number

$1,300 + $250 = $1,550, and the card at 2s says $1,550. The end card says $1,500. The open, the spoken math, and the end card have to be one real number. His end card is the mistake in an otherwise clear reel.
